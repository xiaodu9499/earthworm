# Earthworm Website product baseline

## Product and audience

Earthworm Website is an English sentence practice product for Chinese-speaking learners. It helps learners turn a Chinese prompt into an English sentence through word-by-word typing or sentence reordering, then reinforces pronunciation, vocabulary meaning, sentence structure and familiarity.

The primary users are independent learners using a keyboard on desktop and short, touch-first sessions on mobile H5. A secondary user is a returning learner who expects to resume the latest course and keep progress across devices.

## Core tasks

1. Find a course pack and a suitable lesson.
2. Continue the most recent lesson with minimal navigation.
3. Complete a sentence by typing or reordering words.
4. Listen to the sentence or individual words and inspect vocabulary and structure after answering.
5. Mark a statement unfamiliar or mastered, and revisit learning records.
6. Sign in to preserve progress and familiarity markers across devices.

## Product surfaces

- Home and course discovery: persuasive orientation with direct continuation and clear course browsing.
- Course catalog, account and learning record dialogs: operational interfaces for selection, filtering and account state.
- Practice: an immersive experience surface that keeps the prompt, answer entry and next action inside one viewport.
- Password reset: a focused operational form.

## Constraints

- Course content and totals come from the existing catalog and must not be invented in the UI.
- Local learning remains usable without an account; cloud sync is additive.
- Desktop and mobile have dedicated shells, but share the same course, progress and practice logic.
- Keyboard shortcuts on desktop and touch targets on mobile are both first-class inputs.
